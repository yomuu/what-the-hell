# Needs Kane

Questions only you can answer. Nothing in the app assumes these; your answers go into your CV lines, interview answers and STAR stories.

## Your work at Phone Warehouse (for the "IT support skills that are already security skills" deck)

1. Do you reset passwords or MFA for staff or customers? If so, how do you check who you're talking to first?
2. Do you set up accounts for new starters, change access when people move roles, or remove access for leavers? In which systems (for example Microsoft 365, Active Directory, a point-of-sale system)?
3. Do you write ticket notes? Which ticketing system, and roughly how many tickets a week?
4. Do you wipe and rebuild (reimage) phones or computers? For what reasons, and how often?
5. Do you install updates or patches? Which devices and tools?
6. Do staff or customers report suspicious emails, texts or calls to you? What do you do with them?
7. Do you check backups or restore data, including customer phone data transfers?
8. How many users or stores do you support, and what's your job title?
9. Is there a security moment you're proud of, such as stopping a scam, spotting a fake caller or protecting customer data? That could be your first STAR story.
